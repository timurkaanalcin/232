LiveTrack

Connect to your server

This is the LiveTrack app. It opens the LiveTrack website you already run. Nothing on this screen asks for your location or signs you in.

Use the public address of your LiveTrack deployment. Android emulator to a dev server on this computer: http://10.0.2.2:3000. A phone on the same network uses your computer's LAN address.

  • Sign in with your own account. The app never signs in a guest for you.
  • Location is collected only after you opt in on the sharing screen, then the system permission prompt.
  • While sharing is on, Android keeps a notification on screen with a Stop sharing action.
